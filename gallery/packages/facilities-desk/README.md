# Facilities desk

An app for a facilities or workplace team: anyone reports a problem with its exact location, the coordinator assigns a technician, and the team sees what is open across sites and who is doing what.

## What's inside

| Asset | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Facilities desk** (app) | Four pages: Requests, My requests, Locations, Technicians. Compact sidebar navigation. |
| **Facilities request** (form) | Name, email, location (lookup), exact spot, category, priority, what is wrong, details, photos. |
| **Facilities request** (workflow) | Logs the request, the coordinator assigns a technician and target date, the job is done or closed. |
| **Locations** (data table) | Sites, buildings and floors for the form (8 sample locations). |
| **Facilities requests** (data table) | The request register the cards and charts read (12 sample requests). |

## Pages

- **Requests**: New request (opens the form in a side panel), My requests, KPIs and every request as a searchable card (status badge, priority, category, technician).
- **My requests**: your requests with their status, and the step-by-step timeline of the latest one.
- **Locations** (contributors): requests by site and by building, a site by category pivot and the register grouped by site. A location breakdown without a map.
- **Technicians** (contributors): jobs per technician, requests by status, the list of jobs and assignments waiting, on-time status and requests in versus jobs done per day (Period filter).

## Roles

- **Viewers** browse the requests and charts but never see New request or task buttons.
- **Contributors** report problems and complete the tasks assigned to them. Give everyone this role; the facilities team needs it to work jobs.
- **Editors** change the app in the builder; the person who installs the package is the first editor.

Share the app after install in **Apps → Facilities desk → Settings → Audience**.

## Customise it

- **Locations**: replace the Locations rows; the form's search and fill-in follow at once.
- **Routing**: the install asks who coordinates and who receives the jobs. Point "Do the job" at a technicians team later, or route by category with a condition.
- **Technicians**: edit the names in the Technician choice on the "Assign a technician" step.
- **Look**: change the accent colour, icon or navigation in the app's settings. Status colours come from the theme (success, warning, info), so they work in dark mode.
